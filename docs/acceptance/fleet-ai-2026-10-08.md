## Implemented behavior

### Delayed, bounded fleet radio

LocalBattleSession collects the complete local-observation batch before any AI
controller acts, including human-controlled ship observations. FleetRadioNetwork
copies only local contacts into source-isolated packets. Both teams follow the
same rules:

- 3-second delivery delay, measured from the first actual ingestion of a sample.
- 15-second TTL from the original observedAt, with confidence decay.
- Receiving-ship range recomputation; no live enemy-state lookup or extrapolation.
- No self-report reception, enemy-team sharing, relay of shared reports, or
  reporting from destroyed/test-target ships.
- Missing/destroyed/changed-team sources are removed; unseen target destruction
  does not erase a report and thereby disclose hidden state.
- At most 64 sources, 64 targets per source, two pending plus one delivered packet
  per source/target. Retaining the oldest pending packet prevents starvation.
- Reset and backwards simulation time clear the network.

Reports are separate Observation.sharedContacts. They can guide searching but
cannot increment optical acquisition, populate sensorSnapshots, authorize main
guns/torpedoes, or enter secondary/aviation target feeds. The host inherits the same
LocalBattleSession behavior without new client-trusted fields or hidden-state
replication. Human command precedence and remote-command validation are unchanged.

### Target identity and island withdrawal

Switching to another target clears optical acquisition even inside the same
sampling interval with the same timestamp. RuleBasedAi also clears its old firing
window, range solution and two-sample torpedo track. PlayerPerceptionTracker resets
its own per-target acquisition.

Damaged ships may plan nearby island cover using only the current observed or
remembered threat position. The deterministic planner evaluates 64 straight-path
candidates up to 2 km away, with draft reserve, hull clearance, map bounds and a
post-waypoint stopping corridor. A two-second controller cache bounds planning cost.
Open water and unreachable cover return no plan and preserve normal withdrawal.

Unknown enemy eye height uses the tallest standard hull's static definition
(currently 32 m); own eye height uses own deckHeight + 12. This fixes false cover
behind low terrain without querying an unseen enemy model.

The arrival radius triggers slowdown, not an assumption of cover. Only a
zero-distance plan verifying cover at the current position permits holding.
Physical navigation recovery and torpedo/friendly collision avoidance remain in
control. Cover is evaluated against one known threat, not every possible enemy.
